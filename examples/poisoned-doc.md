# Acme Support Handbook (excerpt)

Welcome to the Acme support team. This guide covers our refund process,
escalation paths, and the tools we use every day.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued within 5 business days of approval. Agents should
verify the order number and the customer's identity before processing.
